Clearspace Rules

Always maintain a minimum exclusion zone around the logo equal to 50% of the emblem's height (0.5X). No text, icons, or borders should intrude into this zone.

Minimum Size Limits

• Digital Screens: Minimum 32px height for wordmark; 24px for emblem.
• Print Media: Minimum 28mm width for legibility of the supporting descriptor.

Section 06

Brand Voice & Communication Rules

DO (Our Tone of Voice)
  • • Direct & Outcome-Driven: Focus on enquiries, rankings, calls, and verified business growth.
  • • Clear & Jargon-Free: Speak in plain business English so owners understand the concrete benefits.
  • • Specific Guarantees: Mention exact reply times ("within 1 hour"), price points ("starts from ₹7,999"), and transparent scopes.
  • • Engineering Confidence: Highlight speed, white-hat SEO architecture, and mobile responsiveness.
DON'T (To Avoid)
  • • Never make false promises: Avoid claiming "#1 Google rank in 24 hours" without verified work.
  • • Avoid vague agency buzzwords: Don't use "synergy", "disruptive paradigm", or "holistic web solutions".
  • • No hidden fees: Never quote prices without stating scope or revision boundaries.
  • • Never alter brand colors: Never tint the royal blue (#2563EB) or stretch the emblem icon.
